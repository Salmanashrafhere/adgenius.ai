# Palette's UX & Accessibility Journal

## 2025-05-14 - Keyboard Shortcut Discoverability
**Learning:** Adding a `<kbd>` hint within search inputs significantly improves power-user discoverability of shortcuts like CMD+K, but it must be visually balanced and hidden on mobile to avoid cluttering the smaller interface.
**Action:** Always include visual hints for keyboard shortcuts in tooltips or directly in the UI (like search bars) to empower users, ensuring they are platform-aware or at least inclusive of major platforms.
